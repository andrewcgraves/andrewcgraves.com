Most of the ceramics spinners that you can get online are expensive. Since its just plastic and a bearing I figured I could make my own and give it away to all my friends.

![the CAD model](https://f004.backblazeb2.com/b2api/v1/b2_download_file_by_id?fileId=4_z8f907fb55c4c22b29911051f_f1113891870894bb9_d20260716_m055125_c004_v0402037_t0029_u01784181085983)

The purpose of a spinner is to place downwards pressure on the piece while trimming away the extra weight. Usually this is just done with your hand or fingers but a spinner lets you place much more pressure without damaging it.

I've put the model on [Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1779909-ceramics-trimming-spinner) for your viewing pleasure.
